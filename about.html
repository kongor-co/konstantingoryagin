What currently has my attention

Technology

I love useful technology: software, hardware and systems that genuinely help people do something better. Right now I am especially interested in AI, automation, robotics and how technology changes the way people work and make decisions.

People

People are complicated, which is exactly why I find them interesting. I read and think a lot about psychology, communication, relationships, leadership, awareness and why we behave the way we do.

Health

I want to live a long life and feel good while doing it. I train regularly, care about mobility, nutrition and recovery, and try to understand my body rather than simply follow the latest health trend.

Self-development

Books, conversations, experiments and new experiences constantly challenge the way I think. I am particularly interested in ideas that I can actually test or apply in real life.

Work

Professionally, I am a technical product manager working in e-commerce.

I am most comfortable with complex problems that sit somewhere between business needs and technical systems: product data, pricing, platforms, integrations, automation and the processes connecting them.

Over the years, I have moved from finance and economic modelling into programming, data and eventually product management.

The phrase that best describes how I think about my work is:

“People. Technology. Business. I work where the three meet.”

I enjoy solving difficult problems, but I care just as much about how people experience the process. Good work should be professional without being miserable. People should feel seen, trusted and appreciated for what they contribute.

For a deeper look at my professional work and projects, visit kongor.co.

Things I'm building

I almost always have a few side projects running.

Right now that includes an Android productivity app and a browser extension inspired by frustrations I have experienced while shopping online.

I also enjoy more physical projects. Sometimes building something means software; sometimes it means designing and constructing a piece of furniture for my home.

The common part is figuring out how something works, discovering what does not work, and trying to make it better.
